About

For the past decade I've built and run the digital backbone of national malaria programs — starting in the field with the Peace Corps and Abt Associates in Uganda, then as a DHIS2 solutions architect supporting surveillance systems across more than fifteen national PMI programs. Today I'm the systems and MEL technical lead for PMI Evolve.

My background sits at the intersection of malaria program work, digital health infrastructure, and evidence generation — I'm as comfortable in a spray campaign's field operations as I am in a database schema or a funder's data request.

I'm currently taking on consulting work in malaria and public health program data systems, and in data visualization and mobile data deployment more broadly.
